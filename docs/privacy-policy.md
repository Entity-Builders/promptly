# Privacy Policy — Promptly

**Last updated:** February 21, 2026

## Overview

Promptly is a desktop application that helps you build, test, and iterate on AI prompts using Google Gemini. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data We Collect

### Analytics (PostHog)

We collect **anonymous usage analytics** to improve the app. This includes:

- Feature usage (which recipes you use, which modes you prefer)
- App version and session info
- Error events (without personal data)

**We do NOT collect:**

- Your prompts, responses, or any content you create
- Your API keys
- Any personally identifiable information (PII)

### API Keys

Your Gemini API key is stored **locally on your device** in the browser's localStorage. It is never transmitted to our servers — it is sent directly to Google's Gemini API.

### Recipes & Settings

All your custom recipes and preferences are stored **locally on your device**. We do not have access to them.

Analytics data is retained for 12 months. You can opt out of analytics by contacting us.

## Your Rights
